Privacy Policy

App name: Pistol Duel Neon

Platform: Android

Developer: MAJI KALI

Effective date: October 31, 2026

In short: Pistol Duel Neon has no accounts, no developer server, and no analytics. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google and its partners may process certain device and advertising information to serve those ads, as described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pistol Duel Neon ("the Game") handles information when you play the Game. The Game is developed by MAJI KALI ("the Developer", "we", "us"). This policy covers information stored by the Game on your device and information that may be processed by third-party services integrated into the Game.

2. What the Game Does Not Do

  • The Game does not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • The Game does not have a developer-operated server, online multiplayer, chat, or user-generated content.
  • The Game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services operated by the Developer.
  • The Game does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or precise location.
  • The Game does not offer in-app purchases.

The only Android permissions the Game requests are internet access (needed to load ads) and vibration (for haptic feedback).

3. Information Stored on Your Device

The Game stores the following information locally on your device so that it can work properly:

  • Gameplay progress, such as the highest level reached, stars earned, and the number of enemies defeated;
  • Unlocked and selected weapons, and other in-game unlocks;
  • Game settings, such as sound and aiming options, and whether you have seen the tutorial.

This information is not uploaded to any server operated by the Developer and is not shared with the Developer. It remains on your device until you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game. Depending on your device and operating system settings, Android's own backup feature may include app data in your Google account backup; this is controlled by your device settings and by Google, not by the Developer.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The Game is free and is supported by advertising. It uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display the following ad formats:

  • Banner ads;
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads, for example after completing a level;
  • App-open ads, shown when you open or return to the Game;
  • Rewarded ads, which you may choose to watch to receive a benefit (see Section 6).

To deliver, measure, and secure these ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and other device identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address;
  • Device and app information (for example device model, operating system version, app name and version, and language);
  • Ad views, clicks, and other interactions with ads;
  • Diagnostic and performance information related to ads, and information used for fraud prevention and security.

Depending on applicable law, your device settings, and the consent choices available to you, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google processes this information under its own privacy policies and terms. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps and websites
  • Google AdMob and Firebase data practices

5. Your Advertising Choices

On Android, you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (usually under Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path may vary by device). Doing so does not remove ads from the Game, but it limits how ads are personalized.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the Game requests your consent for advertising through Google's consent tools before showing personalized ads, and you can review or change your choice when the Game offers that option.

6. Rewarded Ads

The Game may offer optional rewarded ads, for example to try a locked weapon temporarily, to activate a temporary power-up, or to revive after a defeat. Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice. The same advertising data practices described in Section 4 apply to rewarded ads.

7. Third-Party Content and Network Requests

The Game includes its 3D graphics library inside the app. If that bundled file cannot be loaded for any reason, the Game may fall back to loading the same open-source library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs, operated by Cloudflare). In that case your IP address and basic request information are received by that service, as with any internet request. Apart from AdMob and this fallback, the Game does not connect to other third-party services.

Links in the Game, such as the link to this Privacy Policy, open in your browser. Their content is governed by the policies of the site you visit.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

The Developer does not collect your personal information on a server and does not sell it. However, Google and its advertising partners may process information through the advertising services integrated into the Game, as described in this policy. We may also disclose information where required by law.

9. Location Information

The Game does not request or use precise device location. Advertising providers may infer approximate location from your IP address, as described in Section 4.

10. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Developer operates no accounts or data-collection systems through which a child could provide personal information. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information in connection with the Game, please contact us using the details below.

11. Data Retention, Security, and Your Rights

Because the Developer does not hold your gameplay data, there is no developer-held personal data to access, correct, or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by clearing the Game's storage in your Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Information processed by Google and its advertising partners is subject to their own retention and security practices. Depending on where you live, you may have rights over that information (such as access, deletion, or objection to personalized advertising), which you can exercise with Google through its privacy tools and the links in Section 4.

Although reasonable measures are used to protect information, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. International Data Processing

Google and its advertising partners may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Such processing is subject to the providers' applicable privacy policies, terms, and safeguards.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game, its advertising services, or applicable legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised version is published.
