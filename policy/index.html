1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PISTOL NEON ("the Game") handles information when you play the Game. The Game is developed by MAJI KALI. This policy describes information stored by the Game and information that may be processed by third-party services.

The Game may store gameplay progress, game settings, and high scores locally on your device to support its gameplay features.

This locally stored information is not uploaded to a server operated by the developer as part of these local-storage features. It may be deleted if you clear the Game's app data or uninstall the Game.

Depending on applicable law, device settings, and available consent choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms. For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Sharing

The Game does not provide a developer-operated account registration system or multiplayer service.

The developer does not sell personal information directly. However, Google and its advertising partners may process information through the advertising services integrated into the Game, as described in this policy.

5. Location Information

The Game does not require precise device location for its core gameplay, and the developer does not intentionally collect precise location through the Game's gameplay features.

Advertising providers may infer approximate location from an IP address, as described in the advertising section.
